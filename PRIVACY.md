# Privacy: Sunny Block: AI Night (Anna App)

- The app is a static bundle served by Anna. It has no servers of its own and no analytics or trackers.
- For an AI night, a short summary of your recent runs (stars, lit %, gremlins unmasked, mistakes) and the optional wish you type are sent to Anna's LLM through `anna.llm.complete`. Anna handles them under its own privacy policy. We never receive them.
- Game progress and your last 8 AI-night results are stored locally in your browser storage inside the Anna app window and mirrored to your Anna app storage (`anna.storage`) so the Gremlin King can adapt.
- No microphone, camera, location or contacts are used.
